Board Meeting Report

Generates a printable PDF summarising the club's KPIs for the next board meeting. Financials are placed on the final, separate page.

  • 1 Social Media — Instagram, LinkedIn & TikTok reach, growth, engagement
  • 2 Membership — count, year-over-year, new members, tenure
  • 3 Sponsors — paid this year, projected year-end, top partners
  • 4 Financials — income, expenses, net & splits (final page)
